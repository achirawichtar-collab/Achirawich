# Reference formats — news channel, viral selling, CG explainer, mini-drama

Four proven TikTok formats, adapted to the Mazuma signature. Pick one per clip; don't blend two in the first 10 seconds. These are style patterns observed across Thai news channels (Thairath-style), TikTok Shop sellers and TikTok's own creative guidance (Hook → Story → Ending). Copy the *structure and pacing*, never another channel's logo, graphics package, music or footage.

If Tar sends links/screenshots of specific reference clips, study them and add concrete notes to the "Calibration notes" section at the end — TikTok pages can't be scraped from here.

## A. News-channel format ("ข่าวด่วนสไตล์ช่องข่าว")
Use for: water-safety news (arsenic, floods, PM2.5 in water, heat-wave power use), anything with an official source.
- **Frame**: persistent top headline bar (2 lines max, white on dark navy, yellow keyword), small "MAZUMA WATER NEWS"/"WATER LAB" kicker, date chip.
- **0–2 s**: biggest fact as the headline + real footage or news photo (credited) + short sting (t04/t10).
- **2–25 s**: fact → where → who is affected. One fact per shot. Lower thirds for places and sources ("ข้อมูล: กรมควบคุมมลพิษ"). Numbers as Counter style. Maps (satellite CG) for "where".
- **25–45 s**: what it means for your home + what to do (2–3 checklist pills).
- **End**: question to the audience ("บ้านคุณอยู่โซนไหน คอมเมนต์เลย") — news channels drive comments, not hard sales. Product appears as one calm "solution" shot only.
- Voice: steady, newsreader pace (speed 1.1–1.15), no jokes. Captions large, every word.
- Cuts every 1.5–2.5 s; Data Wipe / Glitch / Dip to Navy transitions; low drone bed.

## B. Viral selling story ("เล่าขายของ" TikTok Shop style)
Use for: product clips with a basket (ตะกร้า), promos, comparisons.
Structure (≈20–35 s):
1. **Hook 0–3 s** — a pain the viewer feels *or* a surprising POV ("POV: เพิ่งรู้ว่าน้ำประปาต้มแล้ว…"), face/hand/real home footage, text on screen in the first frame.
2. **Problem 3–8 s** — show it, don't explain it (stained kettle, cold shower, scale).
3. **Product as the turn 8–15 s** — hero reveal (Light Sweep + shimmer), 2–3 features as chips, one demo shot.
4. **Proof 15–25 s** — before/after, a number, real install/use footage, spec from mazuma.co.th.
5. **Offer + CTA 25–35 s** — price/promo only if Tar confirms it; "กดตะกร้าด้านล่าง" with bouncing basket pill.
- Feel: friend-to-friend, energetic, faster voice (1.15–1.2), punchy SFX on each feature, upbeat bed.
- Avoid: fake scarcity, invented prices/discounts, health over-claims.

## C. CG explainer / documentary (Mazuma signature)
Use for: "how it works", maps, science (RO membrane, heat transfer, arsenic path).
- Real-world base (satellite, real footage) + CG on top (extruding provinces, particles, atoms, membranes), cinematic frame + HUD.
- 3-act: question → journey (camera flies through stages) → answer + soft product.
- 55–65 s, cuts 2–3 s, styled transitions at act changes.

## D. Vertical mini-drama (ละครสั้น)
Use for: emotional stories (grandma boiling water, new condo owner).
- Narrator voice + on-screen dialogue bubbles; characters introduced clearly in the first 6 s (name + relation, e.g. "มิ้น · หลานสาวยาย").
- Real footage only (no AI faces mixed with real); same character should look consistent — if footage can't do that, tell Tar.
- Ending: warm resolution + one practical tip + share CTA.
